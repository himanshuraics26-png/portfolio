## Brand & Style

The design system projects an interstellar, immersive, and ethereal sonic journey. It transforms digital streaming into an exploration through deep cosmos, evoking wonder, flow-state focus, and futuristic luxury. The target audience comprises electronic, ambient, synthwave, and audiophile listeners alongside digital-native music discoverers who prize visual atmosphere as an integral dimension of the auditory experience.

The design movement combines **Deep Glassmorphism** with **Atmospheric Space Minimalism**. Rather than flat panels, interfaces manifest as translucent crystalline viewports hovering over deep nebulae. Tactile luminance is paramount: elements do not simply sit upon the background; they absorb, refract, and glow against the void. Subtle chromatic halos, frosted cosmic dust textures, and razor-sharp typographic structures prevent the UI from dissolving into darkness, maintaining precision utility amidst rich spatial depth.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from cosmic nebulae, stellar radiation, and deep gravitational voids.

- **Primary (`#8B5CF6`) & Tertiary (`#A855F7`)**: Radiant violet and neon purple serve as the system's core energy carriers. Applied to primary playback states, dynamic equalizer highlights, progress indicators, and active ambient halos.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`) & Accent Cyan (`#22D3EE`)**: Electric cyan acts as a crisp counter-frequency. Used for live spatial audio badges, high-contrast toggles, stream fidelity markers, and scrubber heads.
- **Cosmic Neutrals (`#070814`, `#0B0E23`)**: Deep obsidian-navy canvas tones ground the interface, avoiding pure sterile `#000000` to preserve chromatic depth when layered beneath translucent frosted cards.
- **Luminescent Highlights**: Translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) drives the frosted glass boundaries, simulating star-lit rim lighting.
- **Typography Neutrals**: Pristine starlight white (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`) for core headings, tempered by diffuse lavender (`#CBD5E1` and `#C4B5FD`) for track metadata, duration trackers, and inactive navigational glyphs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy harmonizes geometric precision with technical instrument aesthetics.

- **Headlines (`Sora`)**: Distinctive geometric apertures with a cosmic, futuristic silhouette. Headlines command structural authority across album viewports and editorial banners.
- **Body (`Outfit`)**: Exceptionally smooth and readable at dense sizes. Carries artist bios, track descriptions, and lyric displays without visual fatigue.
- **Labels & System Meta (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, monoline, and rhythmic. Employed in uppercase tracking for timestamps, audio bitrates (e.g., `FLAC 96kHz/24bit`), pill tags, and navigational breadcrumbs to reinforce the spacecraft-cockpit fidelity.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances expansive interstellar visual canvases with modular utility docks.

- **Grid Structure**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`) transitioning to a 4-column fluid layout on mobile (`margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`).
- **Layout Zones**:
  - **The Celestial Canvas**: Background viewport displaying dynamic, slow-shifting astronomical dust gradients and reactive album artwork reflections.
  - **The Floating Navigation Orb/Rail**: Fixed glassmorphic utility bar anchored laterally on desktop and as a persistent floating dock at the bottom of mobile viewports.
  - **The Sound Horizon**: Persistent player anchored to the bottom edge, using heavy backdrop blur to float seamlessly over content grids without clipping scrolling feeds.
- **Responsive Adaptations**: Dense multi-card carousel grids on desktop collapse into smoothly swipeable horizontal rails with peek edges on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via **Translucent Crystalline Refraction** and **Atmospheric Chromatic Haloing** rather than traditional drop shadows.

- **Glass Substrates**: Surfaces employ background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over a semi-opaque cosmic navy matrix (`rgba(11, 14, 35, 0.65)`). This allows underlaid astronomical art to bleed softly into upper UI surfaces.
- **Starlight Rim Borders**: All cards and panels feature a 1px composite border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` along top and left edges, tapering to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` on lower edges) simulating directional light from celestial bodies.
- **Ambient Chromatic Glows**: Elevated and interactive focal points cast diffuse photon halos:
  - Primary Active Element: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.45)`
  - Cyan Accent Cue: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)`
  - Hover Elevate State: Enhances blur radius and expands the primary color's outer glow by 50% without altering physical scale.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages curvature to emulate spacecraft viewports and celestial lenses.

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: Configured with `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.5rem depending on viewport size), providing an organic yet modern silhouette that softens geometric grid tension.
- **Badges, Action Buttons, and Scrubber Handles**: Strict pill forms (`border-radius: 9999px`) creating distinct touch targets that mirror capsule architecture and glowing laser conduits.
- **Artwork Thumbnails**: Bound to matching container curves with inset starlight borders (`border-white/10`) to seal imagery seamlessly into glass modules.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Playback Buttons**: Circular or pill-shaped, coated in gradient violet-to-neon-purple (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6, #A855F7)`), backed by a persistent ambient purple flare (`0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5)`). Active icons are crisp white.
- **Secondary Action Buttons**: Translucent glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), surrounded by a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`). On hover, rim border transitions to `#22D3EE` with a subtle electric cyan aura.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Controls**: Inset lavender glyphs (`#C4B5FD`) transitioning to radiant white (`#FFFFFF`) with zero container fill.

### Translucent Glass Cards
- Built using `rounded-2xl`, filled with `rgba(11, 14, 35, 0.70)`, and backed by `backdrop-blur-md`.
- Encased in a delicate `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Upon hover, the upper-right corner reflects a faint radial violet flare (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.15), transparent 70%)`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed pill-shaped cosmic obsidian tracks (`rgba(7, 8, 20, 0.85)`), featuring an inset inner shadow simulating depth.
- Border shifts on focus from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` to solid electric cyan (`#06B6D4`), producing a localized cyan aura.
- Placeholder text styled in muted lavender (`#94A3B8`), accompanied by celestial search glyphs (e.g., orbital rings, starlight pins).

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Monospace-styled audio qualities (e.g., `HI-RES LOSSLESS`, `DOLBY ATMOS`) render in `Space Grotesk` with `label-sm` sizing, featuring subtle cyan borders (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.3)`) and dark obsidian backgrounds.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded geometric squares and circles with 1.5px borders in muted lavender.
- Active states flood with `#8B5CF6`, displaying electric cyan check marks and glowing ambient pulses.

### Track Lists & Audio Scrubber
- **Track Rows**: Neutral background on rest; transitions to frosted starlight shimmer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) on hover with rounded-xl corners. Active tracks feature an animated miniature cyan equalizer.
- **Audio Scrubber Bar**: 4px track depth in translucent obsidian (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), filled with a gradient sweep from `#8B5CF6` to `#22D3EE`. The thumb slider is a glowing 12px pill casting a neon halo across the timeline.
